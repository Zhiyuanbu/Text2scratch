  3. Check compiler output before exporting.

    Structure rules

    Before you export

    • Every nested block must close with end.
    • Keep stage-only logic under stage_code =.
    • Declare each sprite before its matching name_code = block.
    • Expressions starting with @ belong inside other commands.
